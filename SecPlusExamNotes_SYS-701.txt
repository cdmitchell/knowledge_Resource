**************N O T E S**********************************************************************
Risk occurs at the intersection of Threat & vulnerability
Risk assessment is a process; performed several ways:
  One-time
   Ad-hoc (conducted in response to a specific event/situation)

  Recurring (regular intervals)
   Continuous (ongoing monitoring)

  Assessed using 2 factors:
    Likelihood/Probability (expressed as a percentage over a period of time)
    Impact

    Risk Severity = Likelihood * Impact

Risk analysis is a formalized approach to risk prioritization
  Qualitative analysis still employs Probability & Impact

4 Risk Management strategies:
  [Acceptance    | Avoidance |  Mitigation| Transference]
   no further RMS| change business practices | apply security controls minimizing probability/Magnitude | Shift impact from Organization

   Acceptance can be in the form of Exception/Exemption

Risk reporting involves communicating status & evolution of risks to Stakeholders;
   should be clear, concise, & focused information to support decision-making

   Examples:
     Dashboard Reporting (graphs & charts)
     Ad Hoc Reports
     Risk Trend Analysis (helps predict possible future risks)
     Risk Event Reports

Business Impact Analysis is a formal process to identify mission essential functions
   4 key meterics:
      Recovery time objective (RTO) amont of time system can be offline before repair
      Recovery point objective (RPO) amount of tolerated data loss during an outage
      Mean time to repair (MTTR)     should be less than RTO
      Mean time between failures (MTFB)

Data subjects are individuals whose data is being processed.

Data controllers determine reasons for processing information & direct the methods for processing data
Data processors processes information on behalf of data controllers.

Right to be forgotten is a concept implemented in various data protection laws, notably the EU GDPR.
  Allows for an individual to request deletion of certain personal data under the following circumstances:
    data no longer needed for its original purpose
    individual withdraws consent
    individual objects & there is no overriding legitimate interests to continue processing
    data has been unlawfully processed
    There is legal obligation to erase the data
